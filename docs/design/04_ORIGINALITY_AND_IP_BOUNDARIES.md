## 3. Forbidden similarities (hard list, from the originality boundaries)

Never use or imitate: Vault-Tec or any corporate-vault fiction; nuclear-vault retrofuturism; blue-and-yellow jumpsuit iconography; bottle-cap or cap-like junk currency; S.P.E.C.I.A.L.-style acronym stats; wrist-device/Pip-Boy-like UI framing; any other game's room names, room-merge rules, or interface layout; existing characters, factions, dialogue, incidents, quests, icons, or lore; a charcoal-photocopy/newsprint monochrome treatment recognizable as This War of Mine's; direct recreation of a real, specific war scenario; existing moral encounters with renamed participants; another game's exact day/night structure; scraped, traced, or unlicensed assets (including unreviewed AI output — see 06, risk R-03).

## 5. Visual-distinction rules (binding on the art stage)

1. **Architecture is testimony:** every space must read as *former public transit* — tile bands, platform edges, signage voids, stair mouths, the stranded train. No corridor-and-cell vault grammar.
2. **Palette law:** interiors live in tungsten amber/soot umber with teal-slate shadow; exteriors in ash-white/heat-haze; night in indigo/ember. **Forbidden:** the blue-and-yellow institutional pairing as any faction/uniform identity; full monochrome charcoal treatment.
3. **Character proportion:** stylized-realistic (~1:6.5 heads), silhouette-first, distinct color keys per resident. **Forbidden:** chibi/bobblehead proportions; photoreal.
4. **UI grammar:** flat, quiet UI with railway-artifact motifs (punch cards, chalk, gauges) *as motifs, not chrome*. **Forbidden:** wrist-device framing, terminal-green retrofuture styling, mascot iconography, any recognizable icon set.
5. **Damage/repair as three readable stages** with restoration visibly warming rooms — progression is *light returning to a station*, an image neither comparison title owns.
6. **AI-assisted concept output (Higgsfield) is reference-only upstream material**: production assets are rebuilt in the controlled modular pipeline; no raw generation ships; every shipped asset traceable to a human-directed source of truth (see risk R-03, R-13).

## 6. Narrative-distinction rules (binding on the narrative stage)

1. No real war, city, or disaster re-created under thin renaming; Harwick and the Char are composites of *climate-era* imagery, not a specific historical event.
2. No moral encounter may be a renamed version of a known scenario from another game; each must grow from Cinderline's own systems (radio triage, gate arrivals, filter economy, rail politics).
3. Factions must have economic logic native to this world (tidal power, tunnel trade, rail control) — no raider/paladin/cult stock trio.
4. The tone contract: catastrophe is climate-era and systemic, never gleeful post-apocalypse kitsch; humor exists but is human-scale (canteen banter, kiosk pedantry), never fourth-wall mascot humor.
5. Character voices, names, and biographies are original creations; no likeness of real persons.
