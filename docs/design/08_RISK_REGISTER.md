# Signal 45 — Production Risk Register

## Feasibility verdict

- **Solo developer:** A graybox and disciplined seven-day slice are plausible only with one station segment, seven reusable room families, five character bodies/one shared rig family, one expedition graph, an authored event spine, placeholder audio/art, and strong tool reuse. A polished full 45-day game at the stated visual/narrative target is **high risk** for one person.
- **Small independent team:** A focused team of roughly 3–6 multidisciplinary contributors is more plausible for the base game, provided the slice proves the architecture and content cost before Act II production.
- **Mobile:** Feasible in concept because the camera and topology are constrained, but 3D cutaways, transparency, lighting, particles, resident animation, overlays, and large shelter span create serious performance/thermal/readability risks.
- **Current evidence:** Design documentation only. No risk is considered closed without the stated prototype or production evidence.

Probability: Low / Medium / High. Impact: Medium / High / Critical.

## Prompt 2 evidence update

Prompt 2 reduces uncertainty but does not close prototype-dependent risks:

- **R-09 Save complexity:** ten save/reload scenarios and unit tests now verify canonical round-trip state, idempotent rewards/refunds, and no offline advancement. Risk remains High/Critical until lifecycle fault injection runs in an app.
- **R-10/R-11 Utilities:** the storm is bounded to one cinder pressure and Air → Power → Water, with no more than three affected systems and one major crisis. Arithmetic passes; phone comprehension and runtime graph performance remain open.
- **R-17 QA state space:** canonical validation, 90 deterministic scenarios, and 67 unit tests now fail on malformed data, route infeasibility, missing components, duplicated rewards, negative stock, and save drift. Device, accessibility, narrative, and runtime state coverage remain open.
- **R-20 Core-loop feel:** modeled ordinary days are 7.5–8.1 minutes, Delegated Day 3 is 9.1, and Active Day 3 is 12.3. This does not lower the probability until instrumented play confirms the timings and visible response.
- **R-21 Route dominance:** East and West pass Full Proof without Highball and finish ten areas; all route/signal, mistake, bounded absence, storm, and Juna paths remain covered. Prompt 3 separates aggregate unused WU from daily slack: both baselines reach zero uncommitted WU on Day 5. West travel and East Water remain asymmetric risks; spatial testing remains the decision gate.

## Risk ownership and review cadence

- The production lead owns the register; discipline owners supply evidence.
- Review at every milestone exit and whenever a feature crosses tiers.
- A probability reduction requires evidence, not optimism.
- A critical risk with a missed decision deadline blocks downstream scope expansion.
- Prototype results, device lists, test seeds, asset-hour measurements, and decisions link back to the relevant Risk ID.

## First five risk burn-down priorities

1. R-20 core loop feel and responsiveness.
2. R-10/R-11 utility legibility and bounded complexity.
3. R-21 deterministic East/West feasibility.
4. R-09 save/offline interruption correctness.
5. R-03/R-04 mobile visual performance and production cost.

These priorities keep production focused on the game’s proof rather than final graphics or content breadth.
