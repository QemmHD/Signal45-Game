## Confirmed requirements versus assumptions

### Confirmed requirements

- Signal 45 title and 45-day campaign target.
- Mobile-first iOS/Android landscape experience, possible later PC.
- 5–12 minute normal shelter session target with safe stopping.
- Side-view 2.5D cutaway using a true 3D world and bounded Room Focus.
- Base-building first, authored shell plus flexible interior adaptation.
- Five stock resources; four utility systems; four primary resident conditions.
- Four starting residents and one possible fifth in the slice.
- Premium or seven-day chapter/full unlock; prohibited exploitative monetization.
- No dangerous simulation while closed.
- No combat power fantasy.

### Assumptions still requiring evidence

- One day can usually fit one normal session.
- Seven room families can express the slice without confusing combined temporary areas.
- Four utilities remain readable and computationally affordable.
- One shared graph can satisfy active and delegated expedition players.
- The grounded 3D target can be produced and rendered within small-team/mobile budgets.
- A 10–14 person community provides enough strategic and emotional variety.
- East and West can be balanced without exact symmetry.
- The seven-day chapter feels complete enough to support the optional commercial structure.
