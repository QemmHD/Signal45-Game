# 13 — FIRST SESSION AND ONBOARDING

**Stage:** Prompt 2. **Status:** LOCKED at beat level; exact copy is the narrative stage's work.
**Contract:** the first session teaches through real play — no encyclopedia screen, no forced tour, no system introduced before it is needed. Timing targets are acceptance criteria — **now actually encoded in 07 §1 (D-042)**, with the measurement protocol defined there (median across the first-time cohort; clock starts at first player input, so the 30-second cold open does not eat the 60-second budget): first meaningful decision ≤ 60 s · first worker assignment ≤ 90 s · first visible repair/construction activity ≤ 2 min · first completed improvement ≈ 4–6 min · first evidence that layout affects people within the session or immediately after · safe autosaved stop by ≈ 8–12 min.

---

## 1. Minute-by-minute (Day 1, guided pace, ≈ 12 minutes)

**0:00 — The cold open (30 s).** The cutaway fades in on the damaged central platform at lamplight: four figures, rubble east, black water west, a dead lift. A short Graymorn-style report (four lines, character-voiced) introduces the residents and the situation: *"Four of us. Water in the tank for three days. The radio heard something last night — a train, in 45 days. Imka says fix the air first."* The Signal 45 hook is planted in one line; nothing about it is asked of the player yet.

**0:30 — Notice and inspect (teach: tap = inspect; pause chip).** The Scrubber Gate's chalk plate pulses (the one live badge). Tapping opens its panel: *"Intake damaged — Air margin 2 days. Repairable."* The pause chip is pointed out once: the world waits while you read.

**0:50 — First meaningful decision (≤ 60 s ✓).** The panel offers the repair works order — cost quoted in plain language (**2 salvage, about half a shift's work** — WU is design vocabulary and never appears on screen, D-042), two staffing options (Imka alone / Imka + Teo faster). One tap to choose, one to confirm.

**1:20 — First assignment (≤ 90 s ✓).** Confirmation bark ("On it."); Imka walks to the Gate, toolbag visible. The works queue chip appears — one entry, progress ring.

**1:50 — Visible work (≤ 2 min ✓).** Sparks, a removed panel, the first stage tick. The player is prompted to try **Fast** once, and sees it drop back to Normal when the next beat arrives.

**2:10 — Second thread: water (teach: HUD forecast).** Maren flags the tank: tapping Water shows the forecast contract — amount, trend, *"≈ 3 days"*, top consumer. The fix offered is a **short install** (purifier cartridge at the tank — a few minutes' work): teaches that not everything is a project. *(A third guided thread — stabilizing the Flywheel — fills the shift's remaining hands: Day 1 is fully productive, per the model.)*

**3:00 — The camp's need (teach: placement class).** Ash asks where the injured stranger cot should go *(placement, 0 WU, undoable)*: two spots — near the warm Flywheel wall (rest bonus, noise) or by the stair (quiet, colder). **First layout-affects-people evidence** — whichever is chosen, a one-line reaction lands that evening.

**4:30 — First completed improvement (4–6 min ✓).** The Scrubber Gate repair completes: fans audibly spin up, the Air warning clears, lights steady — a small **light-return moment**. Imka's bark acknowledges it. *"The station breathes."*

**5:00 — First human reaction.** Teo, passing: *"Didn't think it'd sound like that again."* (+small Strain relief, shown as a gentle chip — the human ledger introduced by feeling, not lecture.)

**5:30 — Survey one blocked direction (teach: RECLAIM workflow).** The player is invited to survey *either* the east rubble or the west flood (the other stays available tomorrow — Day 1 does not force the route choice). The survey report teaches the one-screen section format: contents, hazards, requirements, and its story hook (the shuttered kiosk stall / the deliberate silt line).

**7:00 — EVENING WINDOW introduction (teach: the board, gently).** The radio's **curated Day-1 board: three signals** (first ash front W1, Sable's beacon T1, the Signal 45 carrier V1 — introduced by Maren in one line each), **2 slots**. The choice is real but small; nothing punishes a "wrong" first pick. No nightrun is offered on Night 1 (Teo isn't provisioned yet — tomorrow, says the log).

**8:30 — RETURN & REPORT.** The quiet-shift night resolves in seconds; the morning report shows the day's changes (gate repaired, cot placed, survey filed, what the radio heard), tomorrow's headline (*"Choose a direction — the rubble or the water"*), and the cot reaction line. **Autosave; the marquee safe stop.**

**9:00–12:00 — Optional continuation.** Players who continue flow straight into Day 2's Swelter (the route decision scene — Ash and Imka's advocacy); players who stop leave at a full exhale. Nothing new is introduced until the route scene.

## 3. Tutorial mechanics

- Teaching is **diegetic prompt + highlight**: a resident says the thing; the relevant control pulses once. No tutorial overlay windows longer than two lines; no modal lecture stack.
- Every taught interaction is immediately used for a real outcome (the repair is a real repair; the board is the real board).
- A skip for returning players: "Skip the guided morning" on new-campaign start (restores the Day-1 decision budget as free play; the guided beats' outcomes become defaults). Restart skip logic is budgeted in 03 §3.3's onboarding row.
- Terminology rule: plain label first, canon name second, everywhere ("DAY SHIFT — Swelter"), per D-040.

## 4. Interruption recovery in the first session

The first session obeys the full interruption matrix (12 §3) from minute zero: backgrounding during the cold open resumes the cold open; closing mid-repair resumes at the same works stage; closing on the radio board reopens it un-committed; the Day-1 autosave stop is reached even if the session is fragmented into 2-minute pieces. The guided prompts re-anchor on resume (the last un-acted prompt re-pulses once).

## 5. Acceptance hooks

07 §1's Day-1 gate tests this document's timing targets; 07 §20's probes test its outcomes (players can explain the repair's cause, name the cot decision, and describe what the radio is). The Day-1 decision budget (~8 loops) and the ≈12-minute guided total are model inputs (15) — the first session is costed, not hoped.
