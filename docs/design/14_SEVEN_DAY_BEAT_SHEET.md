# 14 — SEVEN-DAY BEAT SHEET: THE FIRST COUNT

**Stage:** Prompt 2 (regenerated after red-team pass 3, D-042). **Status:** LOCKED at beat level. Every number below is the feasibility model's actual output (15; `tools/simulate_vertical_slice.py`); durations are the session-time submodel's per-day outputs (delegated nights unless noted).

**The week in one line:** hear the promise → choose a direction → win a wing before the storm → hold through it → light the station up → answer Juna at the gate → stand at the relay and decide.

**Numbers note (D-042):** "Available WU" is the model's daily project pool; **routes diverge from Day 5** (west's refund and water-duty drop), so pools are printed E / W where they differ. WU is design vocabulary only — on screen the player sees plain effort language ("about half a shift").

**Route notation:** E: east (comfort first) · W: west (water first). Shared beats carry no prefix. Both routes are model-proven viable with **converged margins (E +18.4%, W +19.9%)** because the human ledger is priced into the labor model (D-042); neither is secretly correct — E buys beds, triage, and the Canteen early at the cost of tank-water drudgery all week; W buys water security and cheap storm recovery at the cost of rough sleeping (a real −1 WU/day) and bench triage until late-week.
