# 14 — SEVEN-DAY BEAT SHEET: THE FIRST COUNT

**Stage:** Prompt 2. **Status:** LOCKED at beat level. Every number below is validated by the feasibility model (15; `tools/simulate_vertical_slice.py`) — "Available WU" figures are the model's computed daily project pools (identical drivers on both routes; competent play), and "Planned WU" is the model's actual allocation. Durations are the session-time model's per-day outputs (delegated nights unless noted).

**The week in one line:** hear the promise → choose a direction → win a wing before the storm → hold through it → light the station up → answer Juna at the gate → stand at the relay and decide.

**Route notation:** E: east (comfort first) · W: west (water first). Shared beats carry no prefix. Both routes are model-proven viable (E +15.5%, W +22.6% margin); neither is secretly correct — E buys beds, triage, and the Canteen early at the cost of tank-water drudgery all week; W buys water security and cheap storm recovery at the cost of rough sleeping and bench triage until late-week.
