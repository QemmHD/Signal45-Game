# Signal 45 — Seven-Day Feasibility

## Verdict

The deterministic Prompt 3-refined model finds **The First Count feasible at baseline but fragile**.

- East and West complete all mandatory slice proofs without Highball.
- Both expand from four to ten functioning areas using seven room families.
- Prompt 3 replaces a single buffer percentage with aggregate, daily, phase, critical-path, incident-reserve, carryover, optional, and emergency-recovery metrics.
- One common 8 WU construction mistake remains recoverable on either route.
- A half-day resident absence remains recoverable.
- Prepared and unprepared-but-responded storms remain recoverable; extended neglect fails.
- Active and Delegated nightruns secure the same required objective from one graph.
- Juna admission, temporary admission, delay, and refusal-with-aid remain viable.
- Deliberately adverse sensitivity and ignored-response cases still fail, so the model does not always pass.

This verdict proves internal schedule consistency only. It does not prove fun, comprehension, emotional impact, camera comfort, visual readability, or actual session duration.

## Day-by-day mini-campaign

### Day 1 — Count the living

Teo's existing respiratory exposure guarantees the Medical teaching path without causing a new injury. The player practices selecting the damaged platform lamp, entering Room Focus, assigning a resident, confirming work, returning to Overview, and reaching a safe save. The lamp and triage cot consume 9 WU and two Materials. The first visible improvement is budgeted at 4.1 minutes.

Stocks, utilities, and signal systems are not taught equally; only one Food forecast, one Power warning, Teo's Health state, and Pause are demonstrated. Everything else is teased or withheld as specified in `15_FIRST_SESSION_AND_SAFE_STOPS.md`.

### Day 2 — Survey before force

The player surveys East and West and sees the actual hazard. Reclamation starts. Good protection and stabilization prevent a new injury. If the player visibly accepts at least two hazards, the deterministic risk score creates a treatable injury. A new injury is never injected merely to demonstrate Medical.

### Day 3 — Commit and go on the wire

The player commits East or West, completes the route reclamation, begins repurposing the concession, and prepares the storm branch. The Harbor Line Maintenance Annex nightrun uses the same node graph in Active or Delegated mode. Its required cage supplies the electrical relay for East or pump seal for West. Retreat after securing it is a partial success.

### Day 4 — Make the route function

East connects utilities, installs quiet berths, and establishes its Water reserve. West connects utilities, restores treatment, and isolates drainage. The alternate signal burden appears: Drainage reduces survey/emergency uncertainty; Trader arrives earlier with Water, Medicine, and an optional radio component. Both combinations remain viable.

### Day 5 — Weather the cinder front

The forecasted storm applies one pressure and at most Air → Power → Water. The player services filtration, sheds load, or isolates Water. Prepared play pays a manageable cost. Partial or unprepared play pays more labor and recovery. Extended neglect can fail. Physical residue and repair state remain visible after the incident.

### Day 6 — Decide who the station can hold

Juna Malek arrives needing aid. Admission costs space, Food, Water, Medicine, and existing-resident attention before it creates any useful work. Prepared admission, temporary admission, delay, and refusal-with-aid are all viable states. Juna is not required for either route's construction schedule or ending eligibility.

The Workshop, Storage area, Triage upgrade, and Forecast Board progress under post-storm pressure. Target misses can carry to Day 7; hard deadlines cannot.

### Day 7 — Choose the next commitment

The station completes visible recovery repairs and a hope beat, reaching ten functioning areas. The ending choice is a direction supported by built state: Prepare the Platform, Root the Settlement, Strengthen the Relay when its optional requirements exist, or Recover First. It is not a morality ranking or a single final switch detached from construction.

## Route distinction

East is spatially more efficient and improves Rest and treatment earlier. Its compact travel budget protects work margin, but Water ends at 15 and a 20% consumption increase fails. West carries more travel and rough-rest burden, but reclamation salvage and treatment output leave Water at 25; the same Water increase remains viable at 12.6.

Neither route receives more functional areas or a secret ending lock. East must retrieve an electrical relay; West must retrieve a pump seal. Both can compensate for their weaknesses through preparation, signal choice, trade, response strategy, and later construction.

## Imperfect play and absence

- East construction rework: viable, 12.1% buffer.
- West construction rework: viable, 11.3% buffer.
- Starting and later cancelling the Platform Lighting Upgrade: viable on both routes; its cost is visible, it adds no second consumer, and it never enters mandatory dependencies.
- Teo absent half of Day 4: viable, 18.2% East buffer.
- Ash unavailable during structural work, Imka during treatment, or Maren during logistics: each changes modeled work and remains viable.
- Teo's respiratory limitation and delayed treatment both consume real capacity and Medicine; neither is an unseen death roll.

The recovery margin is narrow: a global 10% capacity loss fails both routes. “One temporary absence is recoverable” therefore means the bounded tested absence, not arbitrary loss of a resident for the week.

## Nightrun feasibility

The Harbor Annex graph has Gate, Service Passage, Maintenance Cage, Control Booth, Drainage Junction, and Exit nodes. Air protection is required beyond the gate. A service key is preferred; a wrong tool adds time/noise but does not hard-lock the objective. Carry capacity forces the required component, Materials, and optional radio component to compete.

Active uses the same nodes, edges, risk, carry slots, and persistent site state as Delegated. Delegated policy chooses objective priority, risk tolerance, retreat threshold, and tool policy. Active takes 4.8 modeled minutes versus 1.6 delegated minutes and supplies control, not exclusive loot or guaranteed superiority.

## Ending eligibility

- **Prepare the Platform:** route access/survey, signal evidence, survival stock, and no neglected storm.
- **Root the Settlement:** at least nine functioning areas, sufficient rest, survival stock, and recoverable utilities.
- **Strengthen the Relay:** Forecast Board, optional Relay upgrade, radio component, Charge, evidence, and outside contact.
- **Recover First:** survival remains viable while treatment or storm recovery makes ambition unsafe.

Routes influence cost, not moral rank. Multiple directions may be eligible; the selected direction becomes a future plan rather than an instant win.
