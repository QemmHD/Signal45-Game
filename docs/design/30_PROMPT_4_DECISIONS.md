# Signal 45 — Prompt 4 Decisions

Status: final Prompt 4 decision record. Do not interpret validator success as proof of fun or production readiness.

## Prompt 3 corrections

### One canonical Platform Work Lamp

`task_lighting` and `comfort_lighting` were retired. `platform_lighting` is now the single Optional Power consumer. It maps to the Day 1 Platform Work Lamp, is shed in the Relay Load Test, visibly dims, restores automatically, persists through save/reload, and can anchor a minimal hope beat. `platform_lighting_upgrade` modifies the same object/socket and adds no load.

Revisit only if a playable utility tutorial cannot distinguish Power and Charge with one consumer.

### Physical area semantics

The Prompt 3 arithmetic counted a temporary cot and a concession repurpose as new physical areas. Geometry disproved that. The smallest coherent correction removed those increments and assigned distinct-area creation to the route utility room and permanent treatment area. Work, Materials, deadlines, and resident base WU did not change. Both competent routes still derive ten.

A low-capacity sensitivity case that ends below eight areas now supports Recover First when survival and recovery remain viable; it is no longer forced to Proof Incomplete merely because phantom counts were removed. Missing components and actual acceptance failures still produce Proof Incomplete.

### Day 5 travel

Coordinate cost replaces, rather than stacks on, the abstract allowance. East selects 8%/4%. West geometry calculates 8%/4%, but feasibility retains the conservative prior 10% travel pending damp-floor and congestion measurement. This preserves prior expected outcomes without inventing labor or increasing 12 WU resident capacity.

## Coordinate decisions

- Integer `section_id/level/x/lane` addresses are authoritative.
- Sections own authored shell, reclamation, portals, hazards and utility topology.
- Bays express railway spans; cells express placement/navigation.
- Depth lanes are bounded and authored, never free 3D navigation.
- Rendering/world transforms are derived and not saved as authority.
- Dijkstra handles nonuniform portal/lane/carry costs.
- Logical path cost is a proxy, never claimed as seconds.

Rejected alternatives: unrestricted voxel/grid excavation, separate render/placement/navigation coordinate sets, NavMesh-only authority, manual pipe/wire drawing, and engine-specific serialization.

## Building and placement decisions

- Exactly seven slice room families; Power/Air/Water remain Utility specializations.
- Physical areas are unique machine-derived shells, not feature counters.
- Preview and inactive blueprint spend/reserve nothing.
- Activation, delivery, completion, refund and salvage are idempotent transactions.
- Structured invalid reasons include accessible indicators and corrections.
- The slice refuses only-route, trap, essential-room, treatment and evacuation blockers.
- Portal closure uses the same safety gate.
- Modules require an operational parent/socket; no module chains or infinite stacking.

Rejected alternatives: player override for trapping placements, decoration-driven area counts, every utility as a separate room family, and one-off behavior code for every prop.

## Layout evidence

- Day 1: four derived areas; triage is temporary function.
- East Day 7 and alternate: ten areas, seven families, valid access/evacuation.
- West Day 7 and alternate: ten areas, seven families, valid access/evacuation.
- Poor-valid layout: legal but 12% travel/7% hauling, proving inefficiency is measurable.
- West wins pump, isolation and Water-parts routes.
- East wins Rest access and quiet recovery while paying a long gate-Medical route.
- Both routes have physical minimal/full hope anchors.

## Remaining risks

1. Logical cost may poorly predict seconds when animation, lane passing, carrying and congestion exist.
2. The East 28.8 gate-to-Medical carry may feel punitive despite gate stabilization.
3. Day 5 has zero schedule slack; a measured spatial overrun needs siting/staging or project simplification, not extra WU.
4. One gate-to-safe portal remains a known single point of failure until the starting camp clears an alternate passage.
5. Landscape-phone selection, text, safe areas and Room Focus have not been visually validated.
6. True 3D room/prop/character scope remains a major solo-development art and performance risk.

## Prompt 5 entry requirements

Prompt 5 may consume logical room/object IDs, interaction points, path classes and resident location placeholders. It must define residents, aptitudes, traits, work assignment, task reservations, schedules, refusal, emergency orders, promises, memories, sparse relationships, training, recruitment, Juna consequences and population growth.

Before expanding behavior, Prompt 5 must preserve:

- no direct resident control;
- no full collision/steering simulation;
- path requests through the Prompt 4 graph contract;
- assignment eligibility and medical incapacity from Prompt 3;
- no productive labor from self-preservation actions;
- placement/portal safety and save transaction IDs;
- 12 WU base capacity unless a later measured model explicitly revisits it;
- Juna non-mandatory and not free labor;
- four primary conditions, five stocks, four utilities and seven slice families.

Required Prompt 5 prototypes/measurements may be specified, but engine selection, final animation, final art, full relationships, factions, combat, and additional maps remain out of scope.
